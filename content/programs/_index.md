## Community workshops

Free monthly workshops for all levels, from "I've never touched a banjo" to
"help me clean up my drop-thumb." Instruments are available to borrow.

## The field-recording archive

We collect, digitize, and preserve recordings of traditional players — many of
them the last carriers of regional tunes and styles. The archive is open to
researchers, students, and the merely curious.

## Instrument scholarships

Cost should never be the reason a young person can't learn. Our scholarship fund
places open-back banjos in the hands of students who need them, along with a
year of free lessons.
