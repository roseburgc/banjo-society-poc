## Become a member

Membership is open to anyone who loves this music. Members receive our quarterly
tune sheet, early workshop registration, and access to the recording archive.

- **Student / limited income:** $15/year
- **Individual:** $35/year
- **Family:** $50/year
- **Sustaining:** $100/year

## Volunteer

We're run by volunteers, and there's always a role to fill — hosting a jam,
digitizing recordings, helping at workshops, or lending a hand with this very
website.

## Support us

Prefer to give directly? Use the **Donate** button in the corner of any page.
Every gift is tax-deductible and goes straight to our programs.
